The event-list request fields and event-record schema used by recordings.js,
and local database query/table structures used by recordings-local.js,
along with the previously adapted cellular relay handshake, derive from
bropat/eufy-security-client at commit
3bfef130bdcbff8f2f68286a5f63ec7e48177202:
https://github.com/bropat/eufy-security-client
Relevant files: src/http/api.ts, src/http/models.ts, src/http/types.ts, src/http/station.ts,
src/p2p/session.ts and src/p2p/utils.ts.
The bridge uses @mega-yfue/eufy-sdk's existing authenticated transport and
media downloader without copying or replacing that package's implementation.
